## About the Project
A 16-slide visual travel story across Greece (**Thessaloniki**, **Athens**, and **Chania**) created in Figma and turned into a simple Bootstrap 5 carousel website (with no custom JavaScript).

### Slide Order
1. **Intro (Slides 1–3):** Cover page, travel collage, and Greece route map
2. **Thessaloniki (Slides 4–7):** Cover, history, food, and postcards
3. **Athens (Slides 8–12):** Cover, history, streets, food, and culture
4. **Chania (Slides 13–15):** Cover, lifestyle & culture, and local food
5. **Credits (Slide 16):** Team and image credits
